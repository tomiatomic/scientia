Average Vector Value

Because the Fermi surface is spherically symmetric, the average vector velocity over all directions is:

⟨vF⟩=0\langle \mathbf{v}_F \rangle = 0 ⟨vF​⟩=0

due to symmetry (equal probability in all directions).

So the Fermi velocity is about 10–15 times larger than the thermal velocity at room temperature.
