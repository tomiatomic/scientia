Derivation of Sharvin resistance from the Landauer formula:

Physical Setup

  • A Sharvin contact is a small circular aperture of radius aaa connecting two reservoirs.
  • Electrons pass ballistically through the contact (no scattering).
  • The conductance is determined by the number of transverse modes (quantum channels) that can propagate at the Fermi energy.

1. Landauer Formula for Conductance

The Landauer formula relates conductance to the number of quantum channels:

G=2e2h∑nTnG = \frac{2e^2}{h} \sum_{n} T_n G=h2e2​n∑​Tn​

where:

  • TnT_nTn​ = transmission probability of channel nnn
  • G0G_0G0​ = 2e2h\frac{2e^2}{h}h2e2​ is the quantum of conductance (factor 2 accounts for spin degeneracy)
  • For a ballistic contact, Tn≈1T_n \approx 1Tn​≈1, so:

G=2e2hNG = \frac{2e^2}{h} N G=h2e2​N

Thus:

R=1G=h2e2NR = \frac{1}{G} = \frac{h}{2e^2 N} R=G1​=2e2Nh​
